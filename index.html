The person behind the edit

I’m Rubesh.

I’m a freelance video editor working across YouTube, podcasts, promotional content, gaming and social video. I bring structure to raw footage, find the moments worth staying for, and shape them into a polished final cut.

My work covers pacing, story structure, clip selection, subtitles, sound and music, B-roll, color, and motion graphics. I work in DaVinci Resolve and Premiere Pro, adapting each edit to the client’s style and brief.
